# Plan 22 — ambient occlusion in the race (the study's exercise 8): measured, not shipped

**Goal:** screen-space ambient occlusion for the race (RTR 11.3.6; proposal P5), the race's first
composer, P1's shared pipeline module and — since plan 20 — a float depth target with reversed
depth, if the meter says the look is worth its cost.

**Outcome:** the prototype ran, the meter and the pictures were read, and the answer is no: the
effect is hard to see in this scene and costs one to two and a half milliseconds. Nothing is
shipped; P1 keeps waiting for a second composer.

---

## What the prototype did (2026-10-30, main session, M2 Pro, headed Metal, vsync on)

A throwaway `RacePipeline` in the race's canvas: an `EffectComposer` into a half-float target with a
float `DepthTexture` (multisampled below dpr 2, as the arena's), `RenderPass`, three's `GTAOPass`
reading the scene's own depth and reconstructing normals from it (no second scene pass), and
`OutputPass` last; the renderer with `reversedDepthBuffer: true`. It drew correctly: the AO buffer
darkened under the cars, at the barriers' feet and along the kerbs; the composite was the race's
picture, shadows and the sky included, and reversed depth raised no error.

### Two traps in three 0.186

- **`GTAOPass` given a depth texture in its constructor throws**: `setGBuffer` then reads
  `this.normalRenderTarget.depthTexture`, which only the no-texture branch creates. Construct it
  without parameters and call `setGBuffer(depthTexture)` afterwards.
- **The composer draws the scene into its read buffer, a clone of the target you hand it**, with a
  depth texture of its own. Handing GTAO the original target's depth texture made it sample the
  texture of the framebuffer it was writing to: a blank canvas and "Framebuffer is incomplete:
  Attachments are not all the same size". The depth to read is `composer.readBuffer.depthTexture`
  (stable frame to frame while the passes swap an even number of times).
- And for probing: StrictMode calls `useMemo` twice and keeps one result, so a debug handle set
  inside the memo pointed at the discarded composer (1 × 1, never sized). Set it in an effect.

### The cost (race, twenty players, GPU median; single runs except "today")

| | dpr 1 | dpr 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Today, no composer (plan 21, two runs each) | 0.88 / 0.88 ms | 2.16 – 2.63 ms |
| GTAO at full resolution, 16 + 16 samples | 4.00 ms | **22.32 ms** (p95 23.2: the 60 fps check fails) |
| GTAO at half resolution, 8 + 8 samples | 3.34 ms | 3.63 ms |
| …the same without the composer's multisampling | 2.10 ms | — (none at dpr 2) |
| The composer with multisampling, AO off | 1.42 ms | — |

Draw calls 17 → 22 (the composer's passes). At full resolution dpr 2 cost 6.4 times dpr 1 for 4
times the pixels: a tiled GPU storing and reloading full-screen targets between passes. Half
resolution (RTR 11.3.6: AO's cost depends on resolution, not on the scene) cut dpr 2 sixfold. At
dpr 1 the multisampled target's depth has to be resolved for AO to read it, and the two together
cost more than the sum.

### The picture

Three comparisons of one still frame (the countdown, the camera parked), AO blended in and out of
the same render, cropped 1:1 at dpr 2:

- defaults (radius 0.25, scale 1): a little darker behind the rear wheels; otherwise nothing;
- radius 0.5, scale 2: the red barrier block's foot a shade darker; cars unchanged to the eye;
- the AO buffer itself shows the contact clearly — the composite does not, because the cars are
  already grounded by the sun's soft shadow maps — what P5 asked AO for, contact, the shadows
  mostly give here — the asphalt under them is dark, and the Kit's low-poly pieces have few
  creases.

## Decision

Not shipped (*decided by me, for the owner's eye*: the pictures are in the session's scratchpad, and
the prototype is above in enough detail to rebuild in an hour). One to two and a half milliseconds
for a difference visible only in a 1:1 crop is the trade "optimise from a measurement" rules out.
What would change it: a scene lit mostly by the sky rather than the sun, denser geometry with
creases (buildings, stands, foliage), or a phone tier that needs the shadows gone and AO as a cheap
stand-in — each a new measurement, not this one.

With no AO, the race needs no composer, so P1's shared module still has one user and stays unbuilt,
and reversed float depth (plan 20) stays unneeded: nothing fights at today's offset.
