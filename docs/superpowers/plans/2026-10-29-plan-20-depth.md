## What the prototype found (2026-10-29, main session, M2 Pro, headed Chromium on Metal, dpr 2)

- **`TILE_LIFT` is not a depth prop.** It lifts the Kit's tiles, whose root node sits 0.01 low. The
  study doc counted it as one. The only prop is `GROUND_Y = −0.003`: the ground plane under the
  tiles, which would otherwise fight their grass verge.
- **The canvas's depth buffer is 24-bit fixed point** (`gl.getParameter(gl.DEPTH_BITS)` = 24), and
  `EXT_clip_control` is available. Three 0.186 takes `reversedDepthBuffer: true`, but it does not
  give the canvas a float depth buffer, and WebGL cannot: reversed depth pays off only with a float
  depth buffer (RTR 4.7.2), i.e. a render target with `DEPTH_COMPONENT32F` — which the race gets
  when it gets a composer (exercise 8).
- **The step, measured.** The ground painted magenta, the frame taken during the countdown (the
  camera still), magenta pixels counted against a reference with the ground 5 cm down
  (963,490):

  | `GROUND_Y` | near 0.02 (today) | near 0.1 |
  |---|---|---|
  | −0.003 (today) | 965,872 — clean | |
  | −0.001 | 965,882 — clean | |
  | −0.0003 | 965,886 — clean | 965,886 — clean |
  | −0.0001 | **1,038,796 — the verge eaten** | 965,887 — clean |
  | −0.00003 | | 965,888 — clean |

  (The few thousand over the reference at every offset are the ground seen through seams, not a
  fight; a coplanar ground, offset 0, is won by whichever is drawn last and proves nothing.)
  RTR 4.7.2's step for a fixed-point buffer, `Δz ≈ z² (f − n) / (f · n · 2^b)`, is 3 × 10⁻⁴ at the
  verge's ~10 tiles with near 0.02 — between the offset that fought and the ones that did not —
  and five times smaller with near 0.1, which is what the right column shows.
- **The margin today.** The ring is 10 × 9 tiles; the farthest tile either camera sees is 14.8
  tiles away (the overview stands 7 up and 7.5 back from the centre; the chase camera, 1.1 behind
  the car, is bounded at 14.6). There `Δz` = 6.5 × 10⁻⁴: the offset of 3 × 10⁻³ has 4.6 times the
  step. (The planning estimate was 15 tiles and 6.7 × 10⁻⁴; the test computes it.) Nothing
  flickers, so nothing changes; raising `near` would buy margin nobody needs and risk clipping a
  barrier the chase camera passes close to.
